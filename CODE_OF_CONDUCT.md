# Code of conduct

tanGO is a project for people who want to write and run Go web applications. Everyone taking part in its issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to keep the space useful and respectful.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous and assume good faith.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Be specific and constructive when you disagree.
- Respect that contributors have different experience, time and first languages.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about a person's identity.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unsolicited promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who do not follow this code. It applies to the project's GitHub repositories and to any space where someone represents the project.

To report a problem, email me@angvp.com. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.
